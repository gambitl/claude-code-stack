## Trap hit

- `createRunner` in `cstack/src/orca/run.ts` starts every child in the terminal's process group (History, "Questions you may not be asking"). So Control-C also stops `git`, `gh` or `orca`, while cstack keeps running when it has its own `SIGINT` listener. A command that listens for Control-C must treat a cut child as an unknown outcome.
